DiskFinch privacy policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026. DiskFinch is provided by Adam Arulkadacham.

Information processed on your Mac

DiskFinch processes selected-folder paths, file metadata, measured sizes and cleanup results locally. It does not upload scanned files or scan results to us. It includes no advertising, tracking, analytics or developer receipt-verification service.

Selected-folder bookmarks, paths and identity metadata are stored in the app’s sandbox preferences until you forget a grant. Measurements stay in memory and are replaced by a scan or cleared by relevant actions. Forget clears the current scan, selection and review; it does not erase cleanup history.

The local cleanup journal retains original paths, identity metadata, outcomes, returned Trash destinations and whole-volume readings to support review and recovery. It stores up to 20 completed sessions plus a pending session, bounded below 8 MiB. There is no age-based expiry or in-app history erasure. It does not contain file-content backups. Preserve uncertain or unreadable records before managing app data; removing them does not restore files.

Apple purchases

Apple’s StoreKit services load products, verify ownership and handle purchases and restoration. Apple processes account, payment and transaction information under Apple’s privacy policy. DiskFinch uses verified transaction information to determine whether cleanup is unlocked. We do not receive payment-card details. Restore purchases requests synchronization only when you choose it.

This website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no added analytics, advertising, cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening a website sends a browser request; DiskFinch attaches no scan paths, journal or purchase details.

If you contact support through GitHub issues, GitHub and we receive the information you choose to post and your public GitHub profile. Issues are public. Avoid private file contents, unredacted paths and account/payment information. Support issues remain until removed; request removal through that same support route without reposting sensitive information. GitHub’s own retention and copies are governed by its policies.

Your choices and contact

Use Forget to remove a saved folder grant. You control local app data and Trash through macOS. A cleanup record is not a backup. Inspect recovery paths before removing app data; do not erase an uncertain journal to force cleanup. Contact Adam Arulkadacham through the support tracker for policy questions or support-content removal requests.
